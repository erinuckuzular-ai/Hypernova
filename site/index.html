What's inside

Built for low end, tuned for space

  • 362 sounds

    808s, amapiano log drums rebuilt from the FL Studio DX10, subs, reeses, growls, house stabs, techno, dub sirens, keys, pads, strings, bells, voices, arps and a 40-sound Experimental bank.

  • Founders Pack

    22 exclusive sounds, free, installed straight into the preset menu under Sound Packs. Share your own as .hnpreset files and drag them onto the window to import.

  • Wavetable import

    Drop a WAV on an oscillator to build a 32-frame table next to the 15 built-in ones, shown and morphed in 3D.

  • Cross modulation

    Audio-rate FM in both directions, ring mod, AM and filter FM between the two oscillators, plus warps, 8 filter types, 2 LFOs and an 8-slot mod matrix.

  • Effects rack

    Oversampled distortion, OTT, chorus, ping-pong delay, an 8-line reverb with shimmer, EQ, width and mono bass, all driven by macros and LFOs.

  • Updates itself

    Hypernova checks for new versions at most once a day and shows a banner. One click downloads the new installer. You can switch it off in the gear menu.

  • Apple Silicon + Intel

    One universal build for both. macOS 10.13 High Sierra and later (Apple Silicon: macOS 11 and later).

  • Clean install, clean exit

    A branded installer that replaces older versions and keeps your saved sounds, and an uninstaller that asks before touching them. Signed and notarized.

Install

Three steps to orbit

  1. Open the disk image

    Double-click the downloaded Hypernova.dmg.

  2. Double-click Install Hypernova

    Pick VST3, AU, the standalone app and the Founders Pack, then enter your password once. On macOS 10.13 to 11, open Everything else and double-click Install Hypernova.pkg instead.

  3. Rescan your plug-ins

    In Ableton Live: Settings › Plug-Ins › Rescan, then find it under Browser › Plug-Ins › Arrow › Hypernova.

Newer versions install straight over older ones and keep your sounds. To remove Hypernova, open Uninstall Hypernova in the disk image's Everything else folder (macOS 12 and later).
