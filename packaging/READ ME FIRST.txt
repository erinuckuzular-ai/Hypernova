HYPERNOVA

INSTALL
  1. Double-click "Install Hypernova.pkg".
     A newer version installs straight over an older one. Your own sounds are kept.
  2. Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan.
     Find it in Browser > Plug-Ins > Arrow > Hypernova.

FOUNDERS PACK
  22 exclusive sounds, free. The installer adds them to the preset menu under Sound Packs.
  The "FOUNDERS PACK" folder here is the same pack, for sharing or for importing by hand:
  drag the folder (or any .hnpreset file) onto the Hypernova window,
  or use the preset menu > "Import sounds or a pack...".

SHARE YOUR SOUNDS
  Preset menu > "Save current sound..."  (keeps it in My Sounds)
  Preset menu > "Export current sound to share..."  (writes a .hnpreset file)
  Send the file to a friend. They drag it onto Hypernova and it's in their My Sounds.

INSTALLS TO
  VST3   /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Hypernova.vst3
  AU     /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Hypernova.component
  App    /Applications/Hypernova.app
  Pack   /Library/Application Support/Arrow/Hypernova/Packs
  Your sounds  ~/Library/Application Support/Arrow/Hypernova/Presets

UNINSTALL
  Delete the files above.

Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11+.
