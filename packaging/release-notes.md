Download **Hypernova-0.8.0.dmg**, open it and double-click **Install Hypernova**. It installs over any older version and keeps your saved sounds. Signed and notarized by Apple. Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 10.13 and later.

## the fx chain
- **put the effects in any order.** the new fx chain shows all eleven in the order the sound goes through them. drag one somewhere else and the rest slide aside. reverb into distortion, delay before the chorus, the filter last: whatever you like.
- **no clicks.** change the order while a note is playing and it dips for a moment instead of popping.
- **lights and names.** click an effect's light to switch it off. click its name to jump to its controls. effects that are on but not doing anything are dimmed, so you can see what's actually shaping the sound.
- **effect chains.** save the order and every effect setting as a chain and use it on any other sound (CHAINS button). reset the order from the same menu.
- the order is saved with your sounds and sessions, and undo works on it. factory sounds keep the standard order, so they sound exactly as before.
- it's in the effects workspace, and a tab next to the effects everywhere else.
