Download **Hypernova-0.5.0.dmg**, open it and double-click **Install Hypernova**. It installs over any older version and keeps your saved sounds. Signed and notarized by Apple. Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 10.13 and later.

## new in 0.5.0
- **your own wavetables.** drop a WAV on either oscillator (or right-click it). standard 2048-sample wavetable files load as they are; anything else is sliced across its length into 32 frames.
- **cross modulation.** FM in both directions, ring mod, AM and filter FM between the two oscillators, on the Play page.
- **drag to modulate.** drag an LFO, the mod envelope or velocity onto any knob. rings show the depth and move with the modulation. hover a source to see everything it reaches; right-click a knob for the full source list.
- **draggable envelopes.** grab the attack, decay/sustain and release handles directly.
- **fourteen new effects and modes.** flanger, tape wear (wow, flutter, hiss, crackle, saturation), trance gate with patterns, auto-pan, a swept filter, a pitch shifter, chorus modes (ensemble, dimension), reverse and granular delay, and plate, spring and room reverbs. click any effect's name to bypass it.
- **eight noise types.** white, pink, brown, blue, vinyl crackle, tape hiss, digital and wind.
- **stereo view.** goniometer, phase correlation and width by frequency in the Sound Space, which can now fill the window or pop out into its own resizable window.
- **looks.** a new default theme, Paper, plus Cosmic, Blackout, Daylight and Vapor. pick an accent colour, backdrop, knob and panel style in Gear > Look.
- **roomier layout.** taller panels, knob values on hover, bundled fonts.
- **section dice.** re-roll just the oscillators, filter, modulation or effects, and padlock anything you want kept.
- **sound packs.** download packs from the website and drag the zip onto Hypernova.
