## chop lab
- **cut a sample into slices and play them from the keyboard.** press **CHOP** in the sampler: on the hits, or into 4, 8, 16 or 32 equal slices.
- each key from the root up plays its own slice, at the sample's own speed, so a break comes back as it was cut.
- letting go of a key lets the slice play out. **HOLD** stops it with the key instead.
- the cuts are drawn on the waveform with the key each one plays from: drag a cut to move it, alt-click to add one, double-click to take one out.
- the slices are saved with the sound and travel with your presets.

## orbit: morph between sounds
- **capture four sounds into the corners of a square** and drag the point between them. everything continuous blends; a wavetable, a filter type or a switch takes the nearest corner's. two corners morph along a line, three share the square.
- **a corner takes** the sound as it is, the blend you are hearing, or any factory sound: right-click it.
- **the point can travel on its own**: a circle, a figure of eight, a slow drift or a bounce, free-running or in time with the host.
- **morph x and morph y are modulation destinations**, so an lfo, an envelope, the follower or a macro can move it, and the host can automate it.
- **keep blend** writes what you are hearing into the knobs and switches orbit off. while it's live, every knob shows a small mark where the morph has taken it.
- your macros and the machine settings stay where you put them: they aren't part of the morph.
- add it from the widget library (MODULATION > Orbit). the captured sounds travel with the sound, with exported presets, and across an a/b switch.
