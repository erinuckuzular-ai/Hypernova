Download **Hypernova-0.5.1.dmg**, open it and double-click **Install Hypernova**. It installs over any older version and keeps your saved sounds. Signed and notarized by Apple. Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 10.13 and later.

## fixed in 0.5.1
- **the play page was empty.** the arpeggiator, chords, tuning, unison width and cross mod are back.
- **menus.** every pop-up menu now uses hypernova's own look instead of the default grey one.
- **preset browser.** readable in light themes (it was black text on a dark panel).
- **right-click a knob** opens its modulation menu again.
- **theme switching** no longer leaves the old colours in the wavetable views.
- **expanded sound space** is solid and covers the panels properly.
- clipped labels, the gate pattern box, and near-black keys in dark themes.

## lighter on the cpu
- an open editor with nothing playing: from about 11-18% of a core down to about 2%.
- while playing, the 3D views draw roughly half as much per frame and run at 20 fps.

## more 3d
- machined encoder knobs (default in paper), panels that lift off the page, switches that press in, and keys that go down when played.
- the 3D views sway and lean in with the sound, show neighbouring frames and a floor reflection; the orbit view has a comet head; knobs leave a light trail as you turn them.
- the installer, disk image and app icon now match the paper look.
