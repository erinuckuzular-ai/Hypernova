Download **Hypernova-0.3.0.dmg**, open it and double-click **Install Hypernova**. It installs over any older version (including Arrow Bass) and keeps your saved sounds. Signed and notarized by Apple. Apple Silicon + Intel.

## New in 0.3.0
- **Cosmic redesign, animated:** a drifting nebula, twinkling stars and a black hole with a spinning accretion disk behind smoked-glass panels. Drawn on the GPU, so it barely touches the CPU. Settings > Animation: Full / Calm / Off.
- **Lighter on the CPU:** the interface only redraws what changes, and the 3D views rest when nothing plays.
- **320 sounds:** 100+ new ones beyond bass: keys, plucks, pads, strings & brass, bells & mallets, voices, arps, synth drums, cinematic, retro/chip and world.
- **Preset browser:** search, categories, packs, favourites, and every sound auditions as you arrow through.
- **Undo / redo** (Cmd+Z), a **randomiser** that nudges or mutates the current sound, and **window size** options.
- **Uninstaller:** "Uninstall..." in the installer, or the Uninstall Hypernova app in the disk image.
