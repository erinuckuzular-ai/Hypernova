Download **Hypernova-0.11.0.dmg**, open it and double-click **Install Hypernova**. It installs over any older version and keeps your saved sounds. Signed and notarized by Apple. Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 10.13 and later.

## fixes
- **the add-effect menu no longer crashes.** in 0.10.0, opening **+ ADD** in the rack could take the host down with it (the menu read past the end of its own list once crush was added). fixed, and the list can't fall out of step with the effects again.
- **the effects are there when you open it**: the deck along the bottom starts on the rack instead of hiding it behind a tab, and it has the room a unit needs.

## two buses
- **every source picks a bus**: main or alt, on its row in the sources mixer. the two run side by side and meet at the output.
- **every effect sits on one of them**: right-click a unit and move it. its badge says which bus it's on and where it sits in that bus's chain, and the patch leads split into two lanes.
- so a sub can stay clean while the pad goes through the reverb, or one sound can run through two different chains at once.
- the alt bus costs nothing until something plays into it, and about 1.15x one bus when it does. it's saved with the sound.

## speaker
- **speaker**: a new rack unit that plays your sound through a phone, a laptop, a car, a boombox or a club rig. hear what survives before you send the track anywhere.
- **grit** drives the speaker harder, **mix** blends it against the untouched sound, and the display draws the speaker's response over the sound going through it. the mix can be modulated.
