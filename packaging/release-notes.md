Download **Hypernova-0.6.0.dmg**, open it and double-click **Install Hypernova**. It installs over any older version and keeps your saved sounds. Signed and notarized by Apple. Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 10.13 and later.

## arrange it your way
- **every panel is a widget.** grab one by its name and drop it anywhere: in the middle of another panel to make tabs, near an edge to split, along the window edge to run it the full width. a soft outline shows where it will land.
- **no gaps, no overlaps.** hide a panel and its neighbours close up; drag the gap between two panels to share the space differently.
- **bigger panels show more.** give an oscillator, the filter or the envelopes more room and their displays grow instead of everything just zooming.
- **double-click a title** to fill the window with it. right-click a panel to fold, hide, replace or duplicate it.
- **layout mode** (the tiles button) lets you pick up whole panels and opens the widget library.
- **undo and redo** for every layout change. hiding a panel never changes the sound.

## new widgets
- **scope** that locks to the note you play, so the waveform stands still. click to freeze.
- **loudness meter**: short-term, momentary and integrated LUFS, peaks, and a 20 second history with the -14 line.
- **xy pad**: two macros on one pad.
- **mod monitor**: every modulation source that's doing something, live, with what it moves. drag its chips onto knobs.
- **macros**: big macro knobs, rename them in place, see what each one moves.
- **pinboard**: right-click any knob, *pin to pinboard*, and build a panel of the controls you use most.

## workspaces
- **sound design** (the classic layout), **effects** and **analysis** built in; save, rename, duplicate and delete your own. hypernova opens on the last one you used.

## 48 new sounds
- **club studies**: uk garage, dub and dancehall, house bass and stabs, baile funk, club percussion and club experimental, with macros set up for each.

## tidier
- the randomise strip above the effects and the separate stereo view are gone (old sessions still load exactly as before).
