Download **Hypernova-0.4.1.dmg**, open it and double-click **Install Hypernova**. It installs over any older version and keeps your saved sounds. Signed and notarized by Apple. Apple Silicon + Intel.

## New in 0.4.1: runs on older Macs
- **Works on macOS 10.13 High Sierra and later** (Apple Silicon: macOS 11 and later). 0.4.0 was accidentally built for the newest macOS only, so older Macs showed "This Audio Unit plug-in could not be opened" in Ableton.
- **The installer app now runs on macOS 12 Monterey.** On macOS 10.13 to 11, open **Everything else** and double-click **Install Hypernova.pkg**.
- **Opens about 7x faster:** building the wavetables takes around a tenth of a second instead of nearly one second, which matters most on older Intel Macs. The sound is bit-for-bit identical.

Everything from 0.4.0 is included: in-app updates, 40 Experimental sounds, rebuilt 808s, heavier basses, no clicks between notes and a smoother sound browser.
