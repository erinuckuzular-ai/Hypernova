Download **Hypernova-0.9.0.dmg**, open it and double-click **Install Hypernova**. It installs over any older version and keeps your saved sounds. Signed and notarized by Apple. Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 10.13 and later.

## low end
- **a clean sub under any effects.** switch on low end and everything below the split (40 to 300 Hz, drag the line on its spectrum) skips the effects, so you can distort, chorus and reverb the top of a bass while the sub stays solid and centred.
- **sub level, warmth and mono** for the low band on its own.
- **duck**: a tempo-synced sidechain-style pump on just the sub, at 1/4, 1/8, 1/2, a bar or 1/16.
- **phone check**: hear the mix through a phone speaker. it's monitoring only, never part of the sound, never saved.
- no added latency, and switching it on while a note plays doesn't click.

## donks
- **ten new donk basses** in a new *donk & bounce* category: scouse, bamboo, hard house, bounce, rubber, gont, donk + sub, hardbass, pipe and deep house. built the proper way (a two-operator fm "bonk" with a quick pitch blip), with BONK, TONE, DIRT and SPACE on the macros.
- the old donk bass is gone from the browser.

## fx chain
- **scrolls** when it's squeezed into a narrow spot, instead of shrinking the chips. swipe or scroll sideways; drag an effect to the edge and the row scrolls with you.
