Download **Hypernova-0.7.0.dmg**, open it and double-click **Install Hypernova**. It installs over any older version and keeps your saved sounds. Signed and notarized by Apple. Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 10.13 and later.

## a sampler
- **drop a recording anywhere** on hypernova and it plays in the new sampler, straight away: the silence at the start is trimmed and the root note is detected, so it's in tune from the first key.
- **waveform editor**: drag the start and end flags, drag the loop brackets, watch the playhead. double-click a flag to reset it.
- **loop, ping-pong or one-shot**, with a crossfaded loop seam so it doesn't click. **reverse** too.
- **key tracking** on for instruments, off for drums and one-shots. semitone and fine tuning, its own envelope, level and pan, and a switch to send it through the filter.
- **layers with everything else**: the oscillators, sub, noise, filter, modulation and effects all still apply. glide, pitch drop and bend move the sample too.
- **sounds carry their sample.** save or export a sound that uses the sampler and the audio goes with it, so friends never get a missing file.
- WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, M4A, CAF and OGG, up to a minute.

## sampling workspace
- a new built-in layout with the sampler up top and the filter, envelopes, pitch and effects around it.
